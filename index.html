A modern icon

Welcome to the world of Bernico

Defined by sculptural design, her lines flow seamlessly and appear endless. Each Bernico is shaped with purpose and character, created through a highly skilled handcrafted process. On the water, she comes alive. You instantly feel her speed and effortless manoeuvrability. Built for powerful performance without compromise, Bernico represents the highest standard of modern powerboat building.

Nico Bertels built his first boat as a teenager and won his first competition in it. Three decades later the yard still races what it sells — and the leisure boats, the rigid hulls and the RIBs alike, are drawn from those racing hulls.
