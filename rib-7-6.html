Leisure · RIB

Half a metre more, and it changes more than you would expect.

The 7.6 sits between the 7 and the 8, and it exists because that half metre is worth having. More tube, more deck, a little more weight to settle the ride — without moving into a size that needs a bigger berth or a bigger tow vehicle.

01
The extra half metre

More waterline settles the hull through chop and adds usable deck without pushing the boat into the next size class.

02
Central console layout

Walk-around access on both sides, with the helm positioned for visibility standing or seated.

03
Easy boarding

Practical deck flow and boarding from shore or sea — the details that decide whether a RIB gets used every weekend.
