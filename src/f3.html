Racing · Leisure rigid

One hull, two trims — F3 for the water, F3 Xtreme for the course.

The F3 is built in two trims on a single running surface. In leisure trim it is finished for owners; as the F3 Xtreme it is stripped, restrained and set up for competition. What changes between them sits above the waterline.

Photography on this page is a stand-in and does not show this model.

01
Two trims, one running surface

Race trim strips weight and adds restraint systems. Leisure trim adds upholstered seating, storage and finish. The performance underneath is the same.

02
Record practice

A Bernico F3 Xtreme averaged 75.2 mph at Coniston Power Boat Records Week on an epoxy hull under 500 kg.
