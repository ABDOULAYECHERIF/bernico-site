Leisure · RIB

Dominating the waves with raw power and precision.

The RX10 is the RIB most people picture when they think of this yard. A racing running surface under a cockpit finished to a standard that has nothing to do with how RIBs are usually fitted out.

01
A cockpit built like a racing car

Forged-carbon shells on the helm and navigator seats, a carbon hardtop frame overhead and a console laid out around the driver rather than around the dashboard.

02
Instrumented properly

Twin displays at the helm, digital throttle and shift at the right hand, and switching grouped where it can be reached without looking down.

03
Finished, not trimmed

Stitched upholstery, concealed lighting along the gunwale and a bench aft that folds out of the way — detail work carried through to the parts you only notice on the second look.
