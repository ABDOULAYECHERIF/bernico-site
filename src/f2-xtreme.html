Racing · Leisure rigid

The same discipline as the F1, in a hull built to be driven more often.

The F2 Xtreme takes the Xtreme running surface and gives it a little more forgiveness. It races, and it also does the thing an F1 will not — long days, more people, less ceremony. For most owners this is the one that gets used.

01
Broader working range

Trimmed to stay comfortable across a wider band of speed and sea state, rather than being sharpest at one narrow point of it.

02
Deck laid out for people

In leisure trim the cockpit is arranged around movement — boarding, sitting, standing at the helm — without softening the boat underneath.

03
Race-ready when asked

The same hull is delivered in competition specification, with the seating, restraint and rigging changes that go with it.
