Leisure · RIB

Racing DNA in the world of RIBs.

Crafted with Bernico's proven hull design expertise, the 7 is where innovation meets pure excitement. Whether you're cruising coastal waters or pushing performance limits, it delivers an unparalleled experience for up to seven passengers. With options for single or dual engine configurations and constructed entirely from composite materials, it represents the pinnacle of rigid inflatable boat technology.

01
Fast and stable handling

Built for confident driving, with a deep-V hull, a central steering console and a spacious driver's seat for better control.

02
Up to twin 500 HP outboards

Supports high-performance outboard power, with a maximum setup of 2 × 500 HP for strong acceleration and genuine top-end speed.

03
Comfortable seating for seven

Supportive seats, good legroom and a smooth ride — ideal for families, group travel and long journeys.
