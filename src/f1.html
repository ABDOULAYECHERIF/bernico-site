Leisure · Rigid hull

The sharpest hull we build, trimmed for the water you actually use.

The F1 is the point of the range. A deep-V hull built entirely in composite, drawn from competition practice and then trimmed, seat by seat, for owners who want that hull on a Sunday. Nothing about the running surface is softened — only what sits on top of it.

01
Competition deep-V

A fine entry and a hard-run aft section that stays planted through chop at speed, without the slamming that usually comes with that much deadrise.

02
Finished for use

Upholstered seating, storage and a finished cockpit, laid over a hull that was never softened to accommodate them.

03
Composite throughout

Hull, deck and structure are laid up in composite at Willebroek. No wood, no bonded steel — a stiffer platform that keeps its shape season after season.
