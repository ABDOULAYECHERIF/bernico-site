Leisure · Rigid hull

Thirty-five feet, arranged for the whole day rather than the fast part of it.

The VC 35 is the least apologetic leisure boat we build. The extra eight feet over the FTX go into space you use — seating, shade, storage, somewhere to put people who are not currently interested in going fast. It still has the hull underneath it that makes them interested again.

01
Space that is actually usable

Eight more feet than the FTX, spent on deck area and seating rather than on numbers in a brochure.

02
Comfort at the helm

An ergonomic helm position built for long days on the water, with the visibility and support to stay comfortable at speed.

03
Finish that lasts

Premium materials and tight tolerances throughout, chosen so the boat still reads well after several seasons rather than one.
