Racing · Leisure rigid

The offshore race boat, and the largest hull we build.

At 43 feet the Leverage is the biggest thing we build, and the one with the record to prove it. Raced as Bernico–New Star in UIM Class V1 by Nico Bertels and Frank Hemelaer, she took race 1 at the 2014 Grand Prix of Italy in Terracina, race 2 at the 2014 Abu Dhabi World Championship, and won the Venezia–Rimini leg of the 2015 UIM Class V1 World Championship. The leisure trim is the same hull with the finish put back on.

01
A documented race record

Raced as Bernico–New Star in UIM Class V1: race 1 at Terracina 2014, race 2 at the Abu Dhabi World Championship 2014, and the Venezia–Rimini leg of the 2015 V1 World Championship.

02
Length that does the work

Forty-three feet of waterline holds a line through sea states that make shorter hulls negotiate. Less correction from the helm, less fatigue over distance.

03
A usable 43 in leisure trim

The same hull with deck, seating and storage arranged for long days aboard, without giving away the performance the length buys you.
