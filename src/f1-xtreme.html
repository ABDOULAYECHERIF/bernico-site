Racing · Leisure rigid

The sharpest hull we build, offered in race trim and in leisure trim.

The F1 Xtreme is the point of the range. A deep-V racing hull built entirely in composite, set up for competition and then re-trimmed, seat by seat, for owners who want that hull on a Sunday. Nothing about the running surface changes between the two — only what sits on top of it.

01
Competition deep-V

The hull is the constant. A fine entry and a hard-run aft section that stays planted through chop at speed, without the slamming that usually comes with that much deadrise.

02
Two trims, one running surface

Race trim strips weight and adds restraint systems. Leisure trim adds upholstered seating, storage and finish. The performance you feel underneath is the same.

03
Composite throughout

Hull, deck and structure are laid up in composite at Willebroek. No wood, no bonded steel — a stiffer platform that keeps its shape season after season.
