Two families

Rigid, or on tubes

Eleven rigid hulls and nine RIBs. The rigid boats are the racing side of Bernico with the finish put back on — three of them are the same running surface we take to a course. The RIBs trade a little of that edge for forgiveness, capacity and the kind of day where nobody is counting seconds.
